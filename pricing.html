What is not included

Government visa fees, visa-centre service fees, travel insurance, flights, hotels and courier costs are separate. We tell you the expected amounts before you commit.

Changes and cancellations

If your plans change, tell us as early as possible. Deposit refunds and rescheduling are set out in the refund policy.

No approval guarantee

No consultancy can guarantee a visa. The decision belongs to the consulate. We guarantee the quality and completeness of the work we do.
