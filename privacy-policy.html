Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data Visalade collects, why we need it, how long we keep it and the rights you have. Visalade is a trading name of Visalade Limited, registered in England & Wales (company number 00000000).

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Who we are

Visalade is a visa consultancy operated by Visalade Limited, whose business address is Registered Office, London, United Kingdom, EC1A 1BB. For the purposes of UK data protection law, Visalade Limited is the data controller.

You can contact us about privacy at info@visalade.co.uk or by post at the address above.

What we collect

  • Identity and contact data: name, date of birth, nationality, email address, phone number and postal address.
  • Immigration and travel data: passport details, UK immigration status, travel dates, itinerary, accommodation and purpose of travel.
  • Financial data: bank statements, payslips and sponsorship evidence that you provide for the visa application.
  • Application data: forms, photographs, insurance documents, appointment confirmations and correspondence with the consulate or visa centre.
  • Technical data: the pages you visit and basic device information, if our hosting provider records it. We do not run advertising or behavioural tracking.
  • Communications: messages you send by WhatsApp, email, phone or the website forms.

Why we use it

  • To assess which visa you need and whether we can help.
  • To prepare, check and submit your application.
  • To book appointments and communicate with visa application centres on your behalf.
  • To respond to enquiries and provide customer support.
  • To keep records for legal, tax and regulatory purposes.
  • To improve our service and prevent fraud.

Our lawful bases are the performance of our contract with you, your consent where required, our legitimate interests in running and improving the service, and compliance with legal obligations.

Special category data

Visa applications can reveal information about health, religion or family circumstances. We only collect what the application requires, and we handle it with extra care. Where special category data is processed, we rely on your explicit consent or another lawful condition under data protection law.

Who we share it with

We share only what is necessary with:

  • The embassy, consulate or official visa application centre handling your application.
  • Travel insurers, airlines, hotels or invitation hosts, where you ask us to arrange or verify something.
  • Professional advisers, IT providers and payment providers who support our business under confidentiality obligations.
  • Law enforcement or regulators where the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it for advertising.

International transfers

Visa applications are sent to the relevant embassy or consulate, which may be outside the UK. Where data is transferred outside the UK, we take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including the UK's adequacy regulations or standard contractual clauses where required.

How long we keep it

We keep active application files for the duration of the service and for up to six years afterwards for legal, tax and complaint records. Marketing enquiries that do not become clients are kept for up to 24 months. You can ask us to delete data earlier where we do not need it for a legal obligation.

Your rights

  • Access a copy of your personal data.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Ask us to delete data we no longer need.
  • Ask us to restrict how we use your data.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Ask for your data in a portable format.

To exercise a right, email info@visalade.co.uk. We will respond within one month. We may need to verify your identity first.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please contact us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Security

We use access controls, encrypted connections and careful handling of documents. No system is completely secure, but we limit access to the people who need it to prepare your application. If a breach affects your rights, we will notify you and the regulator where required.

Cookies

We use essential cookies only for site functionality, preferences, and session security.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last reviewed.

Questions?

Ask before you commit.

If anything in this policy is unclear, email us and we will explain it in plain language.
