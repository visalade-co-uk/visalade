Why we exist

Visa applications fail for ordinary reasons. A bank statement is three weeks old, a hotel booking does not cover the last night, a middle name is missing from the form, or the applicant applied to the wrong consulate. None of these are dramatic, and all of them are avoidable.

We started Visalade because the process is stressful enough without feeling like you are guessing. Our job is to read the file the way a decision-maker will, tell you what is missing, and handle the administration so you can get on with the trip.

How we work

You get one named consultant, a written fee and a clear checklist. We do not use call-centre scripts or sell appointments we cannot deliver. If something in your file is likely to cause a problem, we say so early, while there is still time to fix it.

We are an independent consultancy. We are not an embassy, consulate, VFS Global, TLScontact, BLS or any government body. The visa decision is made solely by the relevant authority.

Where we are

We are based in Chesham, Buckinghamshire. We work with clients across the UK by WhatsApp, email and phone, and we can arrange in-person support where it helps.
