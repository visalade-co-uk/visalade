Refused a Schengen visa? Read this before you reapply

What a refusal letter actually means, when an appeal is worth it, and how to fix the file for a fresh application.

Schengen refusal letters use a standard form. The consulate ticks one or more reasons, and those reasons are the only useful starting point. Before you book another appointment, read them carefully and match each one to the evidence you submitted.

The common reasons

  • Purpose of travel not established. The itinerary, bookings and cover letter did not add up, or the stated reason was too vague.
  • Insufficient means of subsistence. The bank balance did not support the length or style of the trip, or the money arrived too recently.
  • No proof of accommodation. A night was uncovered, or the booking name did not match the applicant.
  • No proof of return. Employment, study, family or property ties to the UK were thin.
  • Insurance does not cover the trip. Wrong dates, wrong territory or insufficient cover.
  • Information not reliable. Contradictions between documents, or altered evidence.

Should you appeal?

An appeal is usually available where the refusal cites a procedural error or where you believe the decision was wrong on the evidence you actually supplied. It is not a way to add new documents. Deadlines are short and vary by country. If the refusal is about missing evidence, a fresh application is usually faster and stronger.

Do not simply rebook everything

New flight and hotel bookings do not fix a file that was refused for weak financial evidence. Work out which reason applies, then change the evidence. If the trip is no longer realistic, changing the dates or the length of stay can be the honest fix.

What a stronger file looks like

  • A cover letter that addresses the refusal reason directly, without arguing with the consulate.
  • Bank statements covering a longer period, with a clear explanation of any large deposits.
  • Employment or study evidence that shows a reason to return to the UK.
  • Accommodation and travel bookings that match the new dates exactly.
  • Insurance that covers the full trip and all Schengen countries.
