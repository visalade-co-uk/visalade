Not sure which consulate?

The main-destination rule, simply.

You apply to the country where you will spend the most nights. If two countries are equal, apply to the one you enter first.

01

Most nights

Count the nights, not the flights. The country with the most overnight stays receives the application.

02

Equal nights

If two or more countries share the highest number of nights, apply to the country you enter first.

03

Transit

Some nationalities need an airport transit visa even when they stay airside. Check before booking a connection.
