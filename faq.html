Frequently asked questions

We are a private visa consultancy, not a government body. We check which visa you need, build your document checklist, complete and review your application, book your appointment at the visa application centre, and tell you how to track the decision. The embassy or consulate still makes the decision.

No. Visalade is an independent UK consultancy. We are not affiliated with any embassy, consulate, VFS Global, TLScontact, BLS or the EU. You can apply directly yourself; our fee is for preparation, checking and hands-on support.

For short tourism, business or family visits, a UK passport holder can travel to the Schengen area without a visa for up to 90 days in any 180-day period. You will still need to meet the entry rules, including passport validity and travel insurance where applicable. If you hold another passport, the visa requirement depends on your nationality.

Yes. Most of our clients are UK residents travelling on a non-UK passport. We check your immigration status, your eVisa share code or legacy BRP, and the length of your leave to remain, because the consulate will want to see that you can legally return to the UK.

Apply to the country where you will spend the most nights. If you are spending an equal number of nights in more than one country, apply to the country you enter first. If you are only transiting, different rules apply. We confirm the correct consulate before you pay a visa fee.

Our service fees start at £99 for appointment booking, £165 for full application support and £250 for premium support. Government and visa-centre fees are separate and are paid directly to the official channels. We quote everything in writing before you pay.

Yes. Full application support and premium support can be started with a £49 deposit per traveller. The balance is due once your appointment is booked, or before we submit an application that does not need an appointment. There is no interest and no extra fee.

Most applications are decided within 15 calendar days of the consulate receiving the file, and many are faster. Complex files can take up to 45 days. Appointment availability varies by country and season, so we recommend starting four to six weeks before travel.

Yes. We monitor appointment availability at the UK visa application centres for your destination and book a slot in your name. If you already have a slot, we can build the rest of the application around it.

Usually a passport, two recent photographs, the completed application form, travel insurance with at least €30,000 cover, flight reservations, proof of accommodation, bank statements, proof of employment or study, and evidence of your immigration status in the UK. The exact list depends on your destination and purpose of travel; we build a personalised checklist.

No one can. The decision is made solely by the embassy or consulate. What we can do is make sure your file is complete, consistent and presented in the way the decision-maker expects, and tell you honestly if something is likely to cause a problem.

We will explain the refusal reasons and set out your options: an appeal where one is available, a fresh application, or a change of plan. Premium clients get a full post-decision review. Government fees are not refundable, and our refund policy explains which service fees are.
