2. What we do

We provide administrative and advisory support for Schengen visa applications, which may include:

  • Confirming the visa and the correct consulate for your itinerary.
  • Building a document checklist.
  • Reviewing documents for completeness and consistency.
  • Completing or checking application forms and cover letters.
  • Searching for and booking visa appointments.
  • Explaining how to submit the application and track the decision.

3. What we do not do

We do not make visa decisions, influence visa decisions or guarantee that a visa will be granted. The decision is made solely by the relevant embassy or consulate. We do not provide legal advice, and we are not your immigration representative unless we expressly agree in writing.

UK regulatory boundary: Visalade Limited is a private travel and visa consultancy. For UK destinations we provide strictly non-advisory travel logistics (itineraries, reservations, travel insurance) and do not provide regulated UK immigration advice.

4. Your responsibilities

  • Provide accurate, complete and genuine information and documents.
  • Tell us about previous refusals, overstays, criminal convictions or anything else that may affect the application.
  • Meet the deadlines we agree.
  • Attend appointments, biometrics and interviews when required.
  • Pay government, visa-centre, insurance, travel and courier costs directly unless we agree otherwise.

If you give us false or incomplete information, we may stop work. Our fees are not refundable in that situation, and we may be required to report suspected fraud.
