The process

From first message to decision.

A clear, four-step process run by a small UK team. You always know what we are doing, what we need from you and what happens next.

Step 02

We build a checklist for your trip and your nationality. Send each item by WhatsApp or email as you gather it. There is no need to have everything ready before you start.

Step 03

We check and file

Your consultant compares names, dates, balances and bookings across the whole file. We complete the form, prepare the cover letter, book the appointment and tell you exactly what to bring.

  • Line-by-line document review
  • Form and cover letter prepared
  • Appointment booked in your name
Step 04

Attend and track

Attend the appointment if your visa requires one. We explain how to track the decision, what to do if the consulate asks for more, and what your options are if the answer is no.

  • Appointment-day briefing
  • Decision tracking guidance
  • Post-decision support

What we need from you

You do not need a perfect file to start. You need the basics and a bit of time. Most clients send documents over three or four days.

  • Your passport and UK immigration evidence
  • Your travel dates and a rough itinerary
  • Bank statements for the last three to six months
  • Proof of employment, study or business
  • Travel insurance and accommodation bookings
  • Any previous Schengen visas or refusal letters

If something is missing or does not look right, we tell you early — not on the day of the appointment.

How long it takes

A straightforward file can be prepared in two to five working days once we have the documents. The consulate then takes up to 15 calendar days in most cases, or up to 45 for complex files. Appointment availability is the variable we cannot control, which is why we recommend starting four to six weeks before travel.
