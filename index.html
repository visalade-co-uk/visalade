Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on Schengen timelines, UK eVisa share codes, and main-destination rules.

Once you attend your biometric appointment at VFS Global, TLScontact, or BLS, standard Schengen consular processing takes 15 calendar days, though peak summer/holiday seasons can extend up to 30–45 calendar days depending on nationality. We recommend starting 6–10 weeks before your intended travel date.
Under the EU Visa Code, you must apply at the consulate of your main destination—defined as the country where you will spend the highest number of nights. If you are spending an equal number of nights in two or more Schengen states, you must apply through the country of your first point of entry into the Schengen Area. Applying to the wrong consulate results in automatic rejection at the counter.
Yes. Under the Schengen cascade rules, if you have lawfully obtained and used previous Schengen visas within the past two to three years and hold sufficient UK residency validity, your cover letter and application form can formally request a multi-entry visa (6 months, 1 year, or 2 years). Final visa duration always remains at the discretion of the issuing consulate.
With the UK's transition from physical BRP cards to digital UKVI eVisas, Schengen visa centres require a valid UKVI immigration status share code (generated for "other" purposes) alongside your printed eVisa status page showing at least 3 months of remaining UK residency beyond your planned return from the Schengen Area.
A prior refusal does not ban you from reapplying, but you must declare it honestly and address the exact refusal grounds (for example, insufficient financial clarity, unclear itinerary, or employment ties). Our Priority Concierge (£250) and Full Preparation (£165) packages include a structured cover letter addressing previous refusal notices directly.
