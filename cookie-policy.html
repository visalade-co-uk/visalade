Cookie Policy

This site is deliberately simple. We use essential storage only — no advertising, analytics or cross-site tracking cookies.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

What we store

  • Your notice acknowledgement — a local storage entry so the cookie notice is not shown on every visit.

That is the only item this site stores on your device. It is essential to how the notice behaves, and it is not shared with anyone else.

What we do not use

  • No Google Analytics or similar traffic tracking.
  • No advertising or retargeting pixels.
  • No social media tracking buttons.
  • No cross-site profiling.

Third-party content

Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts and images from Unsplash. Those providers may receive your IP address as part of delivering the file. We do not add tracking parameters or share your identity with them. Where you click a WhatsApp link, WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies from that point.

Managing storage

You can clear or block site data in your browser settings at any time. The site will still work; you may see the cookie notice again on your next visit.
