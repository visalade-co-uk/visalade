Document checklist

Everything the consulate expects.

A practical, country-neutral checklist for a Schengen short-stay application from the UK. Your exact list depends on your destination, nationality and purpose of travel — we confirm it before you start.

1. Identity and status

  • Passport valid for at least three months beyond your planned return, issued within the last ten years
  • Previous passports, if they contain Schengen visas
  • UK residence permit, eVisa share code or legacy BRP, if you are not a British citizen
  • Two recent colour photographs to the current Schengen specification

2. The application form

  • Completed, dated and signed application form
  • Names, dates and passport details matching your passport exactly
  • Travel dates that match your bookings

3. Travel evidence

  • Flight reservations showing your name and dates
  • Hotel or accommodation bookings covering every night
  • Invitation letter and host documents, if staying with friends or family
  • A short itinerary for multi-country trips

4. Money

  • Bank statements for the last three to six months
  • Payslips or pension statements
  • Sponsorship letter and sponsor's statements, if someone else is paying
  • An explanation for any large or unusual deposits

5. Employment, study or business

  • Employer letter confirming role, salary and approved leave
  • Self-employment evidence: registration, invoices, tax returns
  • School or university letter for students
  • Retirement or pension evidence

6. Insurance

  • Travel insurance covering the full trip and all Schengen countries
  • At least €30,000 of medical emergency cover, including repatriation
  • Policy dates that start before you leave and end after you return

7. Ties to the UK

  • Proof of address
  • Family, property or study evidence, where relevant
  • A clear reason to return to the UK

8. The cover letter

  • One page explaining who you are, where you are going, why and how it is funded
  • Facts that match the rest of the file
  • An explanation of anything unusual, such as a previous refusal or a gap in employment

Common reasons files are delayed

  • A booking that does not cover the last night
  • A bank statement that is older than the consulate allows
  • A name or date that differs between documents
  • Insurance that covers the wrong dates or countries
  • Applying to the wrong consulate for the itinerary

We check all of the above before anything is submitted. If you would like us to build the checklist for your trip, start an application or send us a message.
