Refund Policy

We want the refund rules to be clear before you pay. This policy applies to service fees paid to Visalade, a trading name of Visalade Limited.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Before work starts

If you cancel before we have started work on your file, we refund your service fee or deposit in full, less any payment processing charge we cannot recover. Tell us in writing as soon as your plans change.

After work has started

Once we have begun, our fee is earned for the work performed. Examples of work starting include requesting documents, reviewing a file, completing a form or searching for an appointment. If you cancel after work has started, we will refund any part of the fee that relates to work not yet carried out, at our reasonable discretion and less any costs already incurred.

Appointment-only service

If we cannot find an appointment for your chosen destination and dates, you can choose a full refund of the appointment-booking fee or ask us to keep searching. If an appointment is booked and you later cancel the trip, the fee is not refundable because the work has been completed.

Government and visa-centre fees

Visa fees, appointment-centre charges, biometric fees, courier charges, insurance premiums and travel bookings are paid to third parties. We do not receive them and cannot refund them. Refunds for those items depend on the third party's own policy.

Refusals

A visa refusal is not a service failure. The decision is made solely by the consulate, and no consultancy can guarantee an approval. Our service fees remain payable for work completed. Premium support includes a post-decision review; it does not include a refund or a free fresh application.

Our error

If we make a material administrative error that affects your application, tell us immediately. We will correct the error at no extra cost and, where appropriate, refund the affected part of the fee. This does not cover errors caused by information you supplied or by a decision of the consulate.

How to request a refund

Email info@visalade.co.uk with your name, application reference, the date you paid and the reason for the request. We will acknowledge within five working days and aim to decide within 14 days.

Chargebacks

Please contact us before raising a chargeback. Most issues can be resolved faster directly. If a chargeback is raised, we may pause work while the payment dispute is investigated.

Questions?

Ask before you commit.

If anything in this policy is unclear, email us and we will explain it in plain language.
