Do I need a Estonia visa?

It depends on the passport you hold, not where you live. British passport holders can visit Estonia for short tourism, business or family trips without a visa. If you hold a non-British passport, you may need a Schengen visa even if you have lived in the UK for years.

Estonia is part of the Schengen area, so the visa also lets you travel to the other Schengen countries during the same trip, up to 90 days in any 180-day period. If Estonia is your main destination — the country where you will spend the most nights — this is the consulate that should receive your application.

If your itinerary takes in several countries, read our guide to choosing the right consulate before booking anything.

Documents for a Estonia visa

  • Passport valid for at least three months beyond your return, issued within the last ten years
  • UK residence permit, eVisa share code or legacy BRP, if you are not a British citizen
  • Two recent Schengen-specification photographs
  • Completed Estonia application form
  • Travel insurance with at least €30,000 medical cover, valid across the Schengen area
  • Flight reservations and accommodation for every night
  • Bank statements for the last three to six months
  • Employer, study or business evidence
  • Previous Schengen visas or refusal letters, if any
  • A short cover letter explaining the trip

This is the country-neutral list. The Estonia centre may ask for additional evidence depending on your purpose of travel. We confirm the exact list before you start gathering documents.

How we prepare a Estonia application

  1. Check the route. We confirm that Estonia is the correct main destination and identify the right visa application centre.
  2. Build the checklist. You get a list tailored to your nationality, status and trip, not a generic PDF.
  3. Review the file. Names, dates, balances and bookings are compared line by line. We tell you what to fix before submission.
  4. Book the appointment. We find a slot at the Estonia centre and tell you exactly what to bring.
  5. File and track. We explain how to submit and how to follow the decision until your passport is returned.

Where to apply in the UK

Applications for Estonia are handled by the official Estonia visa application centre in the UK. The centre may be operated by VFS Global, TLScontact, BLS International or the consulate itself, depending on the country. We confirm the current provider, address and appointment rules before you pay a government fee.

How long will it take?

Most Estonia visa applications are decided within 15 calendar days of the file reaching the consulate, and many are faster. Complex cases can take up to 45 days. Appointment availability is the variable we cannot control, so we recommend starting four to six weeks before travel where possible.
