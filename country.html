Common Questions

France Visa FAQ

How early before my trip should I apply for a France visa from the UK?

You can submit your application up to 6 months before your intended travel date, and no later than 15 working days prior to departure. Peak summer and holiday appointment slots often book out 4–8 weeks ahead.

Which UK city do I attend for France biometrics?

Applications are lodged via TLScontact (London, Manchester, Edinburgh). Depending on consular jurisdiction rules, your appointment location is matched to your UK residential postcode.

What if I am visiting multiple European countries alongside France?

Under the Schengen Code, apply through France if France is your longest stay—or if you spend equal nights across countries, if France is your first port of entry.

Can you help if I previously had a Schengen or UK visa refusal?

Yes. Our Full Preparation and Urgent Priority packages include a line-by-line review of your previous refusal notice so we address every consular concern in your cover letter and supporting evidence.
